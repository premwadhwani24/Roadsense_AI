How RoadSense Works

1. Road data is collected and stored securely with geo-coordinates.

2. Defects and conditions are analysed using AI-assisted models.

3. Risk levels are calculated based on history, severity, and usage patterns.

4. Engineers use the dashboard to prioritise inspections and maintenance.

RoadSense AI is an AI-assisted decision-support system for road infrastructure monitoring.
